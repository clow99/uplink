## Support policy

- **ISP-provided equipment**: full troubleshooting, remote diagnostics, and replacement if defective.
- **Recommended customer-owned**: troubleshooting support for connectivity issues. No replacement — customer must contact the manufacturer for hardware issues.
- **Other customer-owned**: best-effort guidance. Verify the ISP service is working (Ethernet test to modem) and advise the customer to contact the device manufacturer for further help.
- **End-of-life equipment**: inform the customer that their device is no longer supported and may not work with current plan speeds. Offer an upgrade path.
