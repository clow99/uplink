# No Internet Connection

When your internet stops working entirely, here is what you can do before contacting support.

## Quick checks

1. **Check other devices**: try loading a website on your phone, tablet, or another computer. If one device works, the problem is with the device that does not work, not your internet service.

2. **Look at your modem lights**: your modem should have a power light and an online/internet light. If the power light is off, check the power cable and try a different outlet. If the online light is off or blinking, your modem has lost its connection.

3. **Restart your modem and router**: unplug the modem from power. Wait 30 seconds. Plug it back in. Wait 3-5 minutes for all lights to turn solid. If you have a separate router, restart it after the modem is fully back online.

4. **Check cables**: make sure the coax or fiber cable going into your modem is firmly connected. Check both ends if accessible.

## If the problem continues

- If your modem lights look normal but you still have no internet, try connecting a device with an Ethernet cable directly to the modem or router. If that works, the issue is with your Wi-Fi, not your internet connection.
- If nothing works after a full restart, there may be a service outage in your area or an issue with your account. Contact support for further help.
