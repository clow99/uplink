# Slow Internet Speeds

Slow internet can have many causes. Here is how to narrow it down.

## Is it your Wi-Fi or your internet service?

The most important first step is to test with a wired connection. Plug a computer directly into your router (or modem) with an Ethernet cable and run a speed test at fast.com or speedtest.net.

- **Wired speed is good**: your internet service is fine. The slowness is in your Wi-Fi. See the Wi-Fi coverage guide.
- **Wired speed is also slow**: the issue may be with your modem, your plan, or the service in your area.

## Common causes of slow Wi-Fi

- Too far from the router
- Walls, floors, or furniture blocking the signal
- Too many devices connected at the same time
- Using the 2.4 GHz band instead of 5 GHz (2.4 GHz is slower but has longer range)
- Interference from microwaves, baby monitors, or neighboring Wi-Fi networks

## Common causes of slow wired speed

- Your modem may need a restart
- Your modem may not support your plan speed (older modems have speed limits)
- Network congestion during peak hours (typically 6-10 PM)
- A service issue in your area

## What to try

1. Restart your modem and router. Wait 5 minutes and test again.
2. Test from a device near the router to rule out distance issues.
3. Close unnecessary apps and downloads during the test.
4. If it is only slow at certain times of day, it may be peak-hour congestion.
