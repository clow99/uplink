# New Modem Setup Flow

Step-by-step guide for setting up a new modem (cable or fiber ONT) and getting online.

## Step 1: Unpack and identify cables

Your modem box should include:

- The modem unit
- A power cable
- An Ethernet cable
- A coaxial cable (cable internet) or fiber cable information (fiber internet)

Do not connect anything yet.

## Step 2: Connect the signal cable

### Cable internet
1. Find the coaxial outlet on your wall (round connector with a center hole).
2. Screw the coaxial cable onto the wall outlet hand-tight.
3. Connect the other end to the "Cable In" or "RF" port on the back of the modem.

### Fiber internet
1. Locate the ONT (a small box, usually near where the fiber enters your home).
2. If the ONT is already installed and has a green PON light, connect an Ethernet cable from the ONT's LAN port to your router's WAN port.
3. If the ONT needs to be set up, a technician visit may be required.

## Step 3: Power on the modem

1. Connect the power cable to the modem and plug it into a wall outlet. Avoid power strips if possible.
2. Wait 5-10 minutes for the modem to fully boot and connect to the ISP network.
3. Watch the lights: Power should go solid, then Online/Internet should go solid. This means the modem has connected to the ISP.

If the Online light does not go solid after 10 minutes, check cable connections and try a different outlet. If it still won't connect, the modem may need to be activated — contact support with the modem's MAC address (printed on a label on the device).

## Step 4: Connect your router

1. Use an Ethernet cable to connect the modem's Ethernet port to your router's WAN (or Internet) port.
2. Power on the router.
3. Wait 2-3 minutes for the router to start broadcasting Wi-Fi.

## Step 5: Connect your devices

1. On your phone, laptop, or other device, scan for available Wi-Fi networks.
2. Look for your router's network name (SSID). It's usually printed on a label on the router.
3. Enter the Wi-Fi password (also on the router label).
4. Open a browser and load any website to confirm you're online.

## Step 6: Activate your modem (if needed)

Some ISPs require modem activation:

1. Open a browser and navigate to any website. You may be redirected to an activation page.
2. Follow the on-screen instructions. You'll need your account number and the modem's MAC address or serial number.
3. If no activation page appears and websites load normally, the modem is already activated.

## Troubleshooting

- **No lights at all**: verify the power cable is connected and the outlet works. Try a different outlet.
- **Power light on, Online blinking for more than 10 minutes**: the modem can't reach the ISP. Check the coax/fiber connection and call support for activation.
- **Connected to Wi-Fi but no internet**: restart the modem and router in sequence (modem first, wait 5 minutes, then router).
- **Can't find the Wi-Fi network**: make sure the router is powered on and the Wi-Fi LED is lit. Check if Wi-Fi is disabled via a button on the router.
